# Privacy policy — WA Export for WhatsApp Web

All processing happens in your browser. The extension collects nothing and sends nothing to any server: no analytics, no accounts, no remote code.

When you click **Export**, the extension reads only the messages you selected in WhatsApp Web's "Select messages" mode, and their files. It packs them into a ZIP, or a single HTML page if you chose that format, that your browser saves to your computer. The HTML page contains the messages and files themselves and loads nothing from the network when opened. WhatsApp Web itself may download selected files from WhatsApp that it doesn't have cached yet, the same as when you open them. The extension never asks WhatsApp to re-upload expired files, and never sends, forwards, deletes or marks messages.

The only stored data is your export settings (format and message order), kept in your browser's extension storage.
